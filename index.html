About the shop

Serving Nayagarh homes since 2019

Ramachandra Traders opened at Tikiripada, Khandapada, as a small building-material counter. Today we supply tiles, paints, electrical fittings, sanitaryware and interior materials to families, masons and contractors across the district.

Apart from material, our team also takes up complete renovation and decoration work — tiling and flooring, painting and furnishing, doors and wardrobes, balcony work and lighting. You can buy the material and get the work done from the same shop.

Tiles, marble & flooring

Floor and wall tiles, marble, grout and laying work.

Painting & furnishings

Interior and exterior paint, putty, primer and finishing.

Doors, plywood & wardrobes

Ply, laminates, doors and made-to-size wardrobes.

Balcony & lighting

Balcony design, jhumar and light fittings.

Questions people ask us

Still not sure about something? Call the shop — someone is always at the counter between 8 AM and 8 PM.

Do you deliver material to the site?
Delivery depends on the item and your location. Call or WhatsApp with your site address and quantity — the shop will confirm whether delivery is possible, the timing and any charge.
Are the products genuine?
We stock the brands listed on this page through their authorised channels. Ask for a proper bill with every purchase — the manufacturer's warranty applies as per the brand's policy.
Can you calculate how much material I need?
Share your room measurements or floor plan on WhatsApp and we will help you estimate how many boxes of tiles, litres of paint or metres of pipe you need, so you don't overbuy.
What are the shop timings?
We are open all seven days, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. No appointment needed — walk in and ask at the counter.
Can I return or exchange material?
Exchange or return depends on the material, its condition and the brand's policy. Keep the original bill and call the shop before bringing anything back — we will tell you what can be done.
